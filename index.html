Manifesto

The current disruption has generated a blank canvas.

In Mark, ideas are at the core: born free, with the ambition to grow into new brands, companies, or impactful collaborations.

Our purpose is to transcend and generate relevance: to create references that matter today and leave a legacy for tomorrow.

Technology is part of our core, but always led by thought. Because we truly believe AI needs bold human thought to become a force for progress, not regression.

Profit is not the goal, but the natural outcome of thinking and creating on a grand scale, with meaning and humanity.
